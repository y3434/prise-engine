# PRISE Engine — System Design (v7)
### Protein aggregation Regime Inference & Scoring Engine
**Subtitle:** Inference-honest, degeneracy-aware aggregation analytics — hardened across multiple rounds of multidisciplinary senior review

> **Status:** Design / planning document. No implementation. Authoritative specification.
>
> **Scope contract:** PRISE is a *pure-analytical core* — **no runtime integrations** (no live databases, network calls, or pipelines). It ships with a bundled, pre-parsed, **versioned** snapshot of reference data (see §1). Bundled reference data and user uploads are treated identically downstream of ingestion.
>
> **Revision history.** v5 rebuilt the plan to eliminate the major flaws found by four independent senior reviews (biostatistics, amyloid chemical kinetics, structural bioinformatics/database curation, research-software/reproducibility): the reference-data premise was re-scoped to what exists; the self-validating loop was broken with external/adversarial anchoring; the kinetics core was corrected to the modern Knowles/Cohen framework (incl. secondary-nucleation saturation); corpus-scale error-rate control was added; and over-claims ("absolute" score, "bitwise" reproducibility, M8/M9 as shippable modules) were demoted.
>
> **v6 closed two residual flaws a verification pass found in v5**, both attributed to attempting *statistical* calibration on a corpus believed too small to support it — leading v6 to move calibration to synthetic data and drop real-corpus anchoring.
>
> **v7 corrects a FACTUAL ERROR that drove the v5/v6 re-scope.** A direct check of the live CPAD 2.0 database (web.iitm.ac.in/bioinfo2/cpad2) shows it **does** contain a substantial, structured, multi-protein **time-resolved kinetics corpus**: **83,098 aggregation-kinetics data points** (aggregation intensity vs time), with recorded conditions (protein concentration, pH, temperature, mutation, assay method), precalculated aggregation rates (k_agg), **565 aggregation-related structures**, downloadable as CSV/JSON. The v5/v6 premise that "fittable kinetic time-courses barely exist / are order-tens" was **wrong**. v7 therefore: **(1)** restores the kinetics layer to the real CPAD 2.0 kinetics dataset (multiple proteins), not a hand-curated dozen; **(2)** **re-enables real-data statistical calibration and per-stratum anchoring** where strata have adequate n, performed leakage-free (held-out / leave-one-study-out), with **synthetic calibration retained as a complement** (for statistical power, FDR-null validation, parameter-recovery, and regimes under-represented in CPAD) — the v6 "synthetic-only" stance is removed because its justifying premise was false; **(3)** keeps the genuinely valid v4–v6 machinery (artifact gate, censoring, FDR control, identifiability, dual-γ, validity ceilings) intact; **(4)** retains two real, smaller caveats as **build-time audits**: CPAD values are graph-digitized ("marginal error" — propagate an extra uncertainty term), and the per-protein availability of *concentration series* + fine-grained conditions (agitation, ionic strength, ThT level, seeding) must be audited to know how much of the **mechanistic** (Tier-B/γ) pipeline runs vs **descriptive-only**. PDB + CPAD's 565 structures form a **structure-linkage layer** connecting aggregation behavior to protein structure for biological insight.

## 1. Data foundation

> v7: the premise is **validated against the live CPAD 2.0 database**, not assumed. CPAD 2.0 supplies a real, structured, multi-protein kinetics corpus (83,098 time-resolved data points + conditions + k_agg rates + 565 structures, CSV/JSON).

### 1.1 Two-layer bundle
- **Sequence / annotation layer (large, ~thousands of entries).** CPAD 2.0's sequence-side content: curated aggregation-prone regions (912 APRs), 2,031 aggregating/non-aggregating peptides with pre-computed properties (WALTZ-DB/AmyLoad lineage), mutational aggregation-propensity data, behavior tags, and PDB cross-references. Powers: the **sequence axis** (§3-M6), browsing, and the corpus-level missingness/reachability findings.
- **Kinetics layer (real and substantial — CPAD 2.0 kinetics dataset; verified).** CPAD 2.0 holds **82,066 time-kinetics data points merged into 1,781 records** (distinct aggregation-intensity-vs-time curves) across **50+ proteins** — averaging **~46 points/curve**, ample for fitting. Each record carries rich conditions: **protein concentration, pH, temperature, ion name + ion concentration (ionic strength), buffer name + concentration, additives, assay method (ThT/ThS/Congo-red/turbidity/Cytofluor), mutation, PDB-id, PMID**, plus a precalculated **k_agg**. Powers the full kinetic pipeline (M1–M5), and — for proteins with multi-concentration series — global mechanistic fitting and dual-γ.
  - **Authoritative source rule (v7):** ingest **only the full structured CSV/JSON download** per entry — the complete extracted point set. **Never scrape or re-digitize the rendered on-site graph** (a sparse visualization adds a *second* digitization-error layer on top of CPAD's, strictly degrading validity for zero information gain; the CSV is the same data without that extra error).
  - **All CPAD kinetic values are literature-figure digitizations by the curators** (not raw instrument output) → carry an explicit **digitization-uncertainty term** on every CPAD-sourced point (the §1.5 graph-digitization flag).
  - **Per-curve point-density gate (the real validity lever):** record CPAD's per-entry **`Datapoints`** count and the distribution of points through the transition; route each curve via the M1 sampling-adequacy / §4 minimum-effective-points gate — adequate → fittable (descriptive always; mechanistic/γ where a concentration series exists); too sparse → "insufficient transition sampling," **descriptive-only or excluded from mechanistic fitting, never silently fit** (Invariant 4). Re-digitizing an original figure to enrich a too-sparse CSV entry is a careful, flagged, error-bar-bearing **re-curation** task (Phase-2), not graph-scraping.
  - **Build-time audit (W3) — RESOLVED by the implemented ETL** (`etl/cpad_kinetics_etl.py`, run on `full_dataset_17_11_19`; numbers are test-locked). The kinetics file is **long format** (one row per curve×time-point); reconstructing curves by `Time Kinetics` id gives **1,654 distinct curves**, of which **1,526 have ≥6 valid (time,intensity) points** (readily fittable), totalling **81,484 usable (time,intensity) pairs** across **105 distinct proteins**. **Concentration-series census: 52 condition-matched groups across 34 proteins carry ≥3 distinct protein concentrations** → the Tier-B/γ **mechanistically-eligible** sets; everything else is **descriptive-only**. (Time appears in seconds/minutes/hours/days — all normalised to hours by the ETL.) *Still `unknown`-flagged:* agitation, ThT concentration, seeding (no dedicated columns — parse from `additives`/`remarks` where present, else mark unknown for Service A). Data quality varies (some curves sparse/noisy) — exactly what the M1 artifact + point-density gates exist for.
- **Structure-linkage layer.** CPAD's **565 aggregation-related structures** (proteins, fibrils, complexes) + PDB cross-references — used to connect aggregation behavior/regime to structure for biological insight (§3-M5). **PDB access is fully open (verified):** CC0-licensed, no auth/key/CSRF/registration, via documented REST APIs (RCSB data/search/file-download, PDBe, PDBj mirrors). Each CPAD kinetic record's `PDB-id` is the join key, so the structure pull is a trivial keyed fetch. Still **fetched + frozen at build time** (not runtime — §1.4) to preserve the no-runtime-integration invariant and reproducibility; CC0 means the frozen structure metadata is freely redistributable (no author-contact gate, unlike CPAD bulk).
- **User uploads** are first-class and treated identically downstream of M1.

### 1.2 Default experience
The kinetic-analysis pipeline is genuinely supported on the **bundled CPAD 2.0 kinetics dataset** by default — a user can pick a protein and analyze its real time-resolved aggregation data immediately — and on **user uploads**. The sequence axis + structure-linkage + browsing run across the large annotation and structure layers.

### 1.3 Stratification & anchoring (v7)
No multi-axis *combinatorial* stratification (it still fragments to tiny cells). But with the real CPAD kinetics corpus, **real-data per-stratum anchoring is feasible** wherever a stratum reaches an adequate effective n; there it is computed **leakage-free** (held-out / leave-one-study-out; §8) with the anchor's own CI and basin-stability (§7). Where a stratum is too thin, the engine falls back to the **versioned physical/synthetic reference scale** (§3-M6) and returns "cohort N too small" for cohort/percentile output. Which path was used is explicit and versioned. *(This restores the v5 approach now that its data premise is validated; the v6 "drop anchoring / synthetic-only" workaround is removed — see W2.)*

### 1.4 CPAD integration — build-time ETL & schema mapping
> How CPAD's datasets enter the engine **seamlessly** while **capturing the full kinetic data** (not summaries). Consistent with the pure-analytical-core invariant: CPAD is **fetched, parsed, and frozen at build time only** — never queried at runtime.

**Acquire → Transform → Load → Freeze:**
1. **Acquire** the **full structured CSV/JSON export** through CPAD's **legitimate, attributed channel** — its per-dataset/per-protein download form (name + organization) or, for bulk, by contacting the authors (per CPAD's stated policy). *(Verified: the live query endpoint is anti-CSRF + WAF protected and the bulk download is registration-gated — acquisition is therefore a one-time, attributed, build-time step, never runtime scraping, and the CSRF-protected query is never used.)* Never rendered graphs (§1.1 source rule). Capture **every time-point of every record** + all condition columns + k_agg + PMID — the *complete* curve, not the summary or k_agg alone.
2. **Transform — schema mapping (CPAD column → PRISE field):**
   | CPAD column(s) | → PRISE field | Notes |
   |---|---|---|
   | time–intensity table | `AggregationSeries.x[]`, `.y[]` | full-resolution curve; `Datapoints` recorded; digitization-uncertainty attached |
   | Protein concentration | `ConditionVector.concentration` | drives concentration-series grouping |
   | pH / Temperature | `pH` / `temperature` | |
   | Ion name + Ion concentration | `ionic_strength` | |
   | Buffer name + conc / Additives | `buffer/additives` | **parse Additives/Remarks for ThT conc, seeding, agitation** where stated |
   | Method | `assay_type` (+ `assay_reports_mass`) | ThT/ThS → fibril-specific; turbidity → not |
   | Mutation | `construct_id` / `PTM_state` | mutation = a distinct construct (hard branch for comparability) |
   | PDB-id | `structural_statement` + structure-linkage (§3-M5) | per-record structure bridge |
   | PMID / Author / Year / Reference | `field_provenance` (source citation) | |
   | k_agg | precomputed-rate cross-check | compared against the engine's own fitted rate (consistency flag) |
   | (absent columns) agitation / ThT conc / seeding | `unknown`-flagged | Service A missingness policy |
3. **Concentration-series grouping (unlocks mechanism):** group records by `(protein, pH, temperature, assay, mutation, ionic_strength)`; a group with ≥3 distinct concentrations → `concentration_series` (Tier-B/γ eligible); else single curves/replicates → descriptive. This grouping is precomputed at build time, surfaced to the user in **M0**, and is the W3 census.
4. **Normalize & gate:** units harmonized; a.u. intensities marked non-portable; each curve through M1 (baseline, censoring, artifact gate, point-density gate). Heterogeneous-source/assay merges blocked by Service A.
5. **Freeze + version:** the parsed corpus is a frozen, `corpus_version`-stamped artifact (no runtime DB calls). Re-running the ETL on a new CPAD release bumps `corpus_version` → invalidates dependents (§7).

**Seamless-by-design:** downstream of M1, a CPAD-sourced `AggregationSeries` is **indistinguishable** from a user upload — same canonical object, same pipeline, same gates. "Seamless" for the user = M0 point-and-click selection over the pre-grouped CPAD records; "seamless" for the engine = one code path for bundled and uploaded data.

#### 1.4a Verified CPAD kinetics schema (from the downloaded bulk file)
> Confirmed by direct inspection of `aggregation kinetics.xlsx` in CPAD's bulk download (`full_dataset_17_11_19`). This is the concrete parsing recipe.

- **Shape:** one sheet (`Sheet1`), ~83,104 data rows × 33 columns, in **long format** (one row per curve × time-point), **not** one row per curve.
- **Row classes by `Entry` prefix:** **`T-…` = 82,066 time-kinetics points** (the curve data); **`O-…` = 688** endpoint/summary rows; **`R-…` = 350** rate-vs-concentration (k_agg) rows.
- **Reconstruct a curve (T-rows):**
  - **curve id** = the **`Time Kinetics`** integer column (groups a curve's points) → **1,654 curves**;
  - **time** = parsed from the **`Parameter`** text (`"… at X Hours"`); the unit is **seconds / minutes / hours / days** depending on the study — the ETL normalises all to **hours** (this matters: a naïve hours-only parser silently drops ~1/3 of curves);
  - **intensity** = the **`Aggregation Rate`** column (misleadingly named; it holds the per-time-point intensity, *not* a rate);
  - **conditions** (constant within a curve) = `Protein Name`, `Uniprot ID`, **`PDB ID`**, `Mutation`, `Temperature`, `pH`, `Buffer Name/Concentration`, `Ion`/`Ion Concentration`, `Additives`, `Protein Concentration`, `Method`, `Measure`, `PMID`, `Reference`, `Year`.
- **Concentration series** = group curves by `(Protein, pH, Temperature, Method, Mutation, Ion, Ion Concentration)` and collect distinct `Protein Concentration` → **52 groups / 34 proteins** have ≥3 (Tier-B/γ eligible).
- **Other row classes:** `R-…` rows give **k_agg vs concentration** directly (a ready-made scaling dataset); `O-…` rows give per-condition **endpoints** (dose/condition-response, not full curves).
- **ETL output:** each `Time Kinetics` group → one `AggregationSeries` (x=hours, y=intensity, full `ConditionVector`, digitization-uncertainty, `Datapoints`=group size); each `(protein, matched-conditions)` set with ≥3 concentrations → a `concentration_series` for M0.

Emits `curves.jsonl`, `concentration_series.json`, `endpoints.jsonl`, `rate_concentration.jsonl`, `etl_manifest.json` (with source SHA-256 + counts) under `data/processed/` (git-ignored). The manifest's counts are the test-locked numbers above.

### 1.5 Provenance & licensing
A required provenance record carries: source database **version + access date + license + required citation** (CPAD 2.0: Rawat/Prabakaran et al., *Amyloid* 2020; web.iitm.ac.in/bioinfo2/cpad2); **per-entry upstream attribution incl. source PMID** (CPAD records the literature PMID per kinetic entry — extend `field_provenance` to carry it); a **graph-digitization flag + uncertainty term** for CPAD kinetic values; **predictor versions and condition-inputs** for any bundled sequence scores; explicit **redistribution rights** (CPAD 2.0 is open-access/free academic; bulk reuse is "contact the authors" — confirm terms; default to *fetch* rather than *bundle* if redistribution is unclear). **PDB coordinates are CC0 and openly programmatic (verified — RCSB/PDBe/PDBj REST APIs + direct file download, no auth/CSRF/registration), so PDB-derived structure metadata is freely redistributable** — a cleaner path than CPAD's gated bulk download; derived annotations checked separately. ## 3. Core modules M0–M7

> M0–M7 form a coherent, buildable analytics pipeline. M8–M9 (research) are in §5.

### Module 0 — Dataset Selection & Cohort Assembly
**Purpose:** the user-facing entry point. CPAD 2.0 holds **many datasets per protein** across different experimental conditions (concentration, pH, temperature, assay, mutation); M0 lets the user browse by protein and **select one or several real datasets** to analyze — and routes the assembled set to the correct analysis. Mirrors the same flow for user uploads.

- **Availability-bounded selection (no fabrication).** The user filters a protein's *available* CPAD datasets by condition and picks among them. The engine analyzes **real selected data only**; it **never invents a curve for an unmeasured condition.** If no dataset exists for a requested condition, it says so ("no data available"), rather than interpolating a measurement.
- **Assembly → `data_mode` routing (this determines what is scientifically licensed):**
  | What the user assembles | Routed as | Unlocks |
  |---|---|---|
  | one dataset | `kinetic` | descriptive regime + features (single-curve) |
  | same protein + same conditions, ≥2 runs | replicates | random-effects pooling (M1); replicate-scatter signal |
  | same protein, **varying concentration**, otherwise matched | `concentration_series` | **global mechanistic fit + dual-γ + mechanism verdict** |
  | same protein, **varying pH / temperature** (concentration fixed) | condition series | **propensity surface** over the measured range |
  | **multiple conditions co-varying** (e.g. concentration *and* pH) | confounded | mechanism call **refused**; descriptive/surface only, with a confound flag |
- **Validity guards (M0 enforces before anything is fit):**
  - **Comparability check** on the assembled set (same construct/PTM, assay, fibril-vs-non-fibril readout); Service A gates apply. Mixing assays or constructs is flagged, not silently merged.
  - **Confound detection:** if more than one condition varies across the selected set, γ/mechanism is confounded — the set is routed to descriptive/surface and the confound is reported (γ from a series where pH also drifts is not a clean reaction-order signal).
  - **Range-bounded inference:** a propensity surface or γ characterizes only the **measured** range, with a window of validity; **extrapolation beyond the measured conditions is flagged or refused**, and any model-based estimate for an unmeasured condition is labeled an *estimate with uncertainty*, never presented as data.
- **Output:** a validly-typed, comparability-checked analysis set + its `data_mode`, handed to M1.

### Module 1 — Ingestion, Normalization, Artifact Gate, Censoring & QC
- **Source adapters (internal):** `RepositoryLoader` (two-layer bundle — ingests the **full CPAD CSV/JSON export only**, never rendered graphs; carries per-entry `Datapoints` count + digitization-uncertainty), `UserDatasetLoader`. Populate `ConditionVector` with per-field provenance; missing fields flagged, never silently defaulted.
- **Mode detection:** `kinetic` | `concentration_series` (→ global fit) | `dose_endpoint` (the **dominant** bundled non-sequence mode; flagged that it cannot separate "slow" from "low plateau").
- **Per-trace artifact gate** → `fittable | suspect | unfittable` with reasons: photobleaching/decay, baseline & evaporation drift, biphasic settling, inner-filter/quenching (needs `ThT_concentration` — flag when unknown), plate-position systematics, bubbles, insufficient transition sampling. **Non-monotonic traces are never silently fit by a monotonic model.**
- **Censoring classifier:** left (underway at t0 → lag undefined, t50 biased early), right (no plateau → t50 is a *lower bound*; per-trace min–max normalization invalid), interval/irregular. Sets `censoring_class`, `signal_basis`.
- **Normalization discipline:** per-trace min–max flagged plateau-manufacturing; on right-censored / non-conserved-plateau traces it **invalidates** derived t50/γ. A **sub-conservation plateau is also routed as informative** (§M2/M4), not only as an artifact.
- **Plate/experiment-level QC** (distinct from per-trace, with a **control-chart false-alarm budget**, not a fixed tolerance): buffer-only controls flat? ThT-only signal? positive controls within anchor tolerance (doubles as **anchor calibration-drift detection**). Control-less entries → lower quality tier gating confident mechanistic calls.
- **Replicates fit individually**; combined later by **random-effects meta-analysis** (within-fit + between-replicate variance), not curve averaging. Replicate **t50 scatter is retained as a stochastic-nucleation signal** (large scatter ⇒ primary/stochastic-dominated).
- **Bootstrap-appropriate weighting:** heteroscedastic/multiplicative + serially-correlated noise handled by **wild bootstrap** and/or **block/sieve bootstrap along time**, or fitting in a variance-stabilized (log) domain (§4).
- **Implemented:** `engine/m1_ingest.py` (+ `engine/test_m1.py`, 8 passing; `engine/README.md`). Scale-robust triage (decisions use each curve's own range + point-to-point noise, never absolute thresholds). Consumes `curves.jsonl` → `curves_triaged.jsonl` with an `m1` block (fittability_class, censoring_class, recommended_handling, normalization). Real-corpus distribution: **fittable 1,240 / suspect 408 / unfittable 6**; handling **monotonic 1,277 / non-monotonic 323 / descriptive-only 48 / reject 6**; censoring **none 634 / right 156 / left 711 / left_right 142 / interval 11**. *Plate-QC and replicate meta-analysis are N/A for CPAD digitized literature curves (no co-located controls / recoverable replicates) — they activate for user uploads that carry them.* **Thresholds now Service-C-CALIBRATED (thresholds-1.0; GOVERNED apply, 2026-07-01)** — the first-pass `LAG_SLOPE_FRAC` 0.30 (which over-flagged "left/no-lag" ~52%) is superseded by Service C's **held-out** recommendation **0.45** (out-of-sample separation AUC 1.0), sourced from `engine/calibrated_thresholds.py` (constant name unchanged; previous value retained + reversible). Re-triage classification **SHIFT** vs first-pass: censoring `none 634→736 (+102)` · `left 711→607 (−104)` · `right 156→168 (+12)` · `left_right 142→130 (−12)` · `interval 11→13 (+2)`; **fittability (1,240/408/6) and handling (1,277/323/48/6) are unchanged** (the lag-slope gate only touches censoring). Strictly more rigorous (a measured out-of-sample cutoff, not a guess); the change bumps `engine_build_id` (§7).

### Module 2 — Model Fitting Bank (two tiers) + driving-force handling
- **Tier A — descriptive (phenomenological):** linear; polynomial/exponential; segmented/threshold-Hill; logistic; Gompertz; Boltzmann; Hill; smoothing/monotone spline; explicit non-monotonic/biphasic settling model.
- **Tier B — mechanistic (Knowles/Cohen integrated rate laws):** primary nucleation, elongation, secondary nucleation, **saturating secondary nucleation (k₂, n₂, K_M)** [new], and fragmentation forms. **Finke–Watzky** is included but **re-labeled phenomenological autocatalytic** — its constants are *not* amyloid nucleation/elongation rates and are never cross-walked to Tier-B k-values.
- **Conversion-fraction validity windows:** each Tier-B law is fit only within its valid conversion range (early-time linearized vs late-time fixed-point vs full numerical solution); the window is recorded.
- **Global multi-curve fitting** for `concentration_series`: shared rate constants, only monomer varies. The shared-shape constraint partially guards against a *monotonic* ThT distortion but **not a concentration-dependent** one (e.g. inner filter at high m), which corrupts γ — flagged.
- **Driving-force handling:** where estimable, m_crit is a **jointly-fit nuisance parameter** (not preprocessing), with its identifiability profile and its **anti-correlation with γ** reported; supersaturation `ln(m/m_crit)` offered as an alternative parameterization with sensitivity reporting. **Where the range doesn't approach m_crit, m_crit is unconstrained and the correction is cosmetic** — nominal-m fallback is a flagged validity downgrade.
- **Assay-semantics layer:** each assay declares fibril-specificity **and** a **mass-proportionality flag**. Mechanistic models are licensed only on fibril-reporting assays (ThT/ThS), and ThT-derived rate constants are tagged "valid under ThT∝mass (unverified)." Turbidity/DLS/SLS (also sensitive to amorphous/LLPS/gelation) cannot mint mechanistic claims.
- Robust fitting: bounds, **data-seeded (deterministic) multi-start** — the starts are constructed from the data, *not* drawn from an RNG, so there is no seed to pin here; the fit provenance records this as `rng: not applicable` with a reason rather than inventing one (§2.3) — graceful (flagged) non-convergence, with **failed starts now retained in the basin ledger** instead of silently skipped.
- **Implemented (Tier A + Tier B mechanistic + dose–response):** `engine/models.py` (closed-form registry), `engine/mechanistic.py` (Knowles/Cohen ODE family), `engine/m2_fit.py` (driver). Tests: `test_m2.py` (12) + `test_tierb.py` (5), all passing — parameter recovery across the bank. Three axes:
  - **Kinetic (vs time):** `lnt` (linear no-threshold), `logistic`, `gompertz`, `richards` (generalised-logistic sigmoid), `exponential`, `scaling_law` (power), `brain_cousens` (biphasic/hormesis), `finke_watzky` (Tier-B closed form). Routed by M1 `recommended_handling` (monotonic sigmoid never forced onto a non-monotonic curve). Real corpus (1,654 curves, ~2.4 min, 176/~11.5k non-converged ≈ 1.5%): preliminary best-by-AICc gompertz 327 / exponential 290 / brain_cousens 264 / lnt 232 / richards 184 / logistic 178 / scaling_law 103 / finke_watzky 70.
  - **Dose–response (vs concentration):** `dr_lnt`, `dr_threshold` (hockey-stick), `dr_hill` (4PL), `dr_brain_cousens`, `dr_scaling` — fit the concentration-series / R-row (k_agg-vs-concentration) data. *(Brain–Cousens & LNT are dose–response models by nature; this is their native home, and they are also exposed on the kinetic axis.)*
  - **Tier B mechanistic (vs time):** one **ODE framework** (P, M moment equations; exact LSODA integration, no error-prone closed forms) covering `nucleation_elongation` → `secondary_nucleation` → `saturating_secondary` (k₂,n₂,K_M) → `fragmentation`, fit in log-rate space. These are **sloppy** — a synthetic secondary-nucleation curve fits at R²≈1.0 but individual rates are *not* uniquely recovered (only stiff combinations are). This is demonstrated in the tests and is exactly what M3's identifiability gate + M4/global-γ are for.
  - *AICc here is preliminary — principled, bootstrap-enveloped, identifiability-gated selection is M3.* **Global concentration-series fitting + the γ scaling exponent + m_crit/driving-force handling are now implemented in M4** (master-curve global γ + censored t50-regression γ; the full Knowles/Cohen *ODE* global fit remains the documented refinement).

### Module 3 — Comparison, Selection & Identifiability (bootstrap-enveloped)

In brief: model selection runs **inside** the resampling envelope; selection is **within-tier** (descriptive vs descriptive, mechanistic vs mechanistic), and the descriptive↔mechanistic comparison uses a **cross-validated predictive criterion**, not a shared AIC table; **effective sample size = number of independent curves/conditions**, not serially-correlated time points. Outputs: selection frequencies, per-model conditional distributions, identifiable-combination report, FDR-controlled decisions.
- **Implemented:** `engine/m3_select.py` (+ `engine/test_m3.py`, 8 passing). The non-identifiability cutoff `COND_NUMBER_NONIDENT` is now **Service-C-calibrated (thresholds-1.0): 1000 → 107** (held-out separation AUC 1.0) — the round first-pass 10³ guess is superseded by the measured ~10² boundary (previous value retained in `engine/calibrated_thresholds.py`; bumps `engine_build_id`, §7). ### Module 4 — Feature Extractor, Definition Contract & Dual-γ
- **Primary anchor: `t50`** (half-time), tagged with `signal_basis` and `censoring_class` (value vs inequality).
- **Lag time** always reported with its definition attached. The **lag-to-t50 ratio** (a dimensionless shape invariant) is a first-class feature — one of the few mechanistic signals available from a *single* curve.
- Inflection, max rate, plateau, transition sharpness, dynamic range — per versioned **definition contract**. Cross-protein aggregation requires **definition-homogeneity** (a Gompertz t50 and a tangent-intercept lag are not comparable).
- **γ provenance defined precisely as the half-time scaling exponent** (distinct from the lag-time exponent; they map to reaction orders differently).
- **Two γ estimators, separately provenanced, never merged:** `gamma_global` (from the Tier-B global fit) and `gamma_regression` (censored errors-in-variables regression of log t50 on log driving-force; §4). Disagreement is reported as signal, with an **operational disagreement test** (non-overlap of bootstrap CIs beyond a stated margin).
- **γ is a constraint, not a discriminator** (see §3-M5): it constrains a linear combination of reaction orders and is **many-to-one** onto mechanism.
- **Curvature test** re-labeled: significant log-log curvature ⇒ *"reaction order is concentration-dependent — consistent with secondary-nucleation saturation, approach to m_crit, OR mechanism transition"* → routed to the global fit; reported with its power ("curvature undetectable at this design's power" when underpowered).
- Every feature carries bootstrap uncertainty + validity flags.
- **Implemented:** `engine/m4_features.py` + `test_m4.py` (11 passing). Versioned **definition contract** `m4-defs-1.0` attached to every payload; features read off the *selected* model on a dense grid (definition-homogeneous). Censoring-aware `t50_status` (point / lower_bound / biased_early / irregular) and a **non-cooperative guard** (no interior inflection ⇒ lag & inflection `None` + flag, never an arbitrary argmax). Dual-γ: `gamma_regression` (**censored** log–log ML with OLS no-censoring fast path; curvature, informative-censoring (§S3) and m_crit-identifiability (§K4) diagnostics; **reliability gate** ≥3 uncensored anchors + physical |γ| + finite CI, diverged fits flagged not faked) and `gamma_global` (**master-curve collapse** coupling all member curves through one shared γ — the v1 global estimator; full Knowles/Cohen ODE global fit deferred). Operational **disagreement test** (CI non-overlap, gated on regression reliability). Real corpus: 1,217/1,240 fittable curves yield full feature vectors; **lag-to-t50 ratio median 0.71 (IQR 0.58–0.81)** across the 473 cooperative curves (nucleation-dependent signature). Dual-γ over the 52 concentration series (build-time batch ≈7 min; bootstrap auto-scales down for large series): 19 yield a γ, **13 pass the reliability gate**, the disagreement test fires on 2, and 1 is blocked for informative censoring. Clean agreeing examples: Aβ42 γ_reg ≈ 1.07 (CI 0.94–1.14) / γ_global 1.10; α-synuclein γ_reg ≈ 0.15 / γ_global 0.18; Ure2 0.31 = 0.31. *Deferred: ODE global fit for γ_global, separate left-censoring algebra (left-only t50 excluded from γ), explicit EIV concentration-error term.*

### Module 5 — Classification & Regime Registry (degeneracy- & shape-aware)
- **Regime Registry** — declarative, hierarchical, versioned. Top-level regimes: *no detectable aggregation (assay-sensitivity-qualified)* / *gradual non-cooperative (downhill)* / *threshold-driven* / *cooperative sigmoidal* / *non-monotonic / settling-confounded* / *anomalous / unclassified (open-world)*.
- **Mechanistic sub-regime calls are gated on the global-fit curve shape** (early-time apparent rate + lag-to-t50 ratio), with γ as a **consistency check only** — never sole evidence.
- **Three outputs:**
  1. **Descriptive regime** — always available.
  2. **Mechanistic verdict OR data-regime-conditional equivalence class** — the equivalence class is **drawn from Service C's confusion matrix computed for the actual data regime** (single-concentration vs clean series vs saturating), not a hard-coded `{secNuc, frag}` pair. Single-mechanism calls refused above a degeneracy threshold; the threshold is FDR-controlled (§4).
  3. **Confidence + rationale + condition vector + window of validity.**
- **Agitation and seeding are hard mechanism branches** (quiescent vs shaken can change the dominant nucleation pathway; quiescent-γ and shaken-γ never share a propensity surface).
- **`structural_statement` is recorded, not used as a primary stratifier**; fibril **polymorphism** is a first-class caveat ("the structure of protein P" can be ill-posed). **Structure-linkage layer:** fitted regimes/mechanisms are linked to CPAD's 565 aggregation-related structures + PDB cross-references to surface structure↔behavior biological insight (e.g. APR/structural context of the fastest-aggregating regimes) — reported as an *associative* insight panel, never as a causal claim from a single structure.
- **Model-agnostic anomaly detector** (residual structure the model bank cannot represent) — FDR-controlled; feeds the human-governed proposer. **Only manual approval mutates the registry, and every mutation is a signed, dated, rationale-bearing, version-bound artifact** (§7).
- **Implemented:** `engine/m5_classify.py` + `test_m5.py` (26 passing). Declarative, hierarchical, **versioned** `REGIME_REGISTRY` (`m5-regimes-1.0`). (1) **Descriptive regime** is pure shape and always available, never erased by the anomaly channel (`anomalous_unclassified` reserved for no-fit curves). (2) **Mechanistic** output **refuses single-mechanism calls** and returns an equivalence class + the gates it failed — assay-licensed, **shape-gated** (γ consistency-check-only, K1), **agitation/seeding hard branch**, **data-regime-conditional degeneracy** (broad→reduced-by-γ→re-broadened-by-curvature, K2/K3); the Service-C confusion-matrix class + FDR degeneracy threshold are flagged deferred, not faked. (3) **Anomaly detector** = Wald–Wolfowitz runs test on best-fit residuals → corpus **Benjamini–Hochberg FDR** → human-governed **proposal** artifacts (`PROPOSED_PENDING_HUMAN_APPROVAL`, signed/dated/version-bound) that never auto-mutate the registry. Plus an **associative** CPAD/PDB structure-linkage panel (polymorphism caveat). Real corpus (1,240 fittable), first-pass-threshold build: cooperative_sigmoidal 611 · gradual_non_cooperative 502 · threshold_driven 79 · no_detectable 48 (611+79 = 690 = M4's interior-inflection count — cross-module consistency). **Mechanistic licensed on 0 curves** (agitation/seeding `unknown` corpus-wide — the design's D5/hard-branch concern, made empirical). ### Module 6 — Propensity Scorer (surface + reference-anchored intrinsic + cohort) & Sequence axis
```
Propensity {
    surface   { feature vs driving-force|condition, gamma_global+CI, gamma_regression+CI, window_of_validity }  // PRIMARY where data allow
    intrinsic { value, condition_vector, anchor_kind(corpus|reference_scale), anchor_ref, anchor_effective_n?, anchor_CI?, anchor_version }
    cohort    { value, rank, percentile, cohort_ref, N }   // within a condition+assay-matched stratum or the cohort under analysis; "N too small" suppresses it
}
```
- **Intrinsic anchoring (v7) is two-path and explicit:** where a condition+assay-matched **CPAD stratum reaches adequate effective n**, the score is a **corpus-referenced** position within that stratum (carrying `anchor_effective_n` + `anchor_CI`, derived leakage-free per §8); where it does not, it falls back to the **versioned physical/synthetic reference scale**. `anchor_kind` records which was used. Either way it is "deterministic given `anchor_version`," **not** "absolute/stable across conditions" — valid only at its stated `condition_vector`.
- **Cohort** is computed within a condition+assay-matched CPAD stratum (where one exists with adequate N) **or** the **cohort under analysis** (e.g. a user-supplied multi-protein screen); suppressed ("N too small") for singletons. Cross-condition / cross-assay ranking remains blocked (Service A).

## 4. Statistical inference spine

> Consolidated so the inferential claims are auditable. This is the methodological backbone for M3–M6.

- **Partial-pooling hierarchical estimation spine** (replicates → protein → stratum). m_crit and rate constants are **latent**; censoring enters the likelihood directly; replicate combination is a built-in random-effects layer. Partial pooling shrinks unstable per-curve estimates toward stratum means — the right response to sparse per-protein data. **Determinism is preserved by deterministic approximate inference** (Laplace / fixed-seed variational) **with convergence gates (R̂/ESS analogues)** rather than free-running MCMC — see §7 for the reproducibility trade-off, stated openly.
- **Model uncertainty via Bayesian model averaging / bagging of *predictions*, not parameters.** A **marginal predictive interval is emitted only for cross-model functionals** (t50, the observable propensity-surface value at a condition); model-specific micro-parameters (k_n, k₊, k₂, λ) are reported **per model, conditional on selection**, alongside **selection frequencies**. Any marginal interval discloses its **mixture/multimodality** structure. *(Fixes the ill-defined "pooled selection-conditioned CI.")*
- **Selection inside the resampling envelope:** each resample re-runs gate → fit → identifiability → select. Reported: selection-stability frequencies + selection-conditioned conditional distributions. Resampling uses **wild and/or block/sieve bootstrap** appropriate to heteroscedastic, serially-correlated kinetic noise; **BCa** intervals for skewed parameters; a **minimum-effective-points gate** below which CIs are declared unreliable (point estimate only).
- **Error-rate control where multiplicity exists:** repeated decisions (regime, mechanism-resolvability, γ-curvature, anomaly, plate-drift) are framed as tests against **Service C synthetic nulls** and subjected to **Benjamini–Hochberg / Benjamini–Yekutieli FDR control, stratified** so the null is exchangeable within stratum. A target FDR is stated and reported. **Multiplicity is real chiefly in the large sequence layer** (thousands of predictor calls) **and in user screens / synthetic test batteries**, not in the dozens-of-proteins kinetics layer — FDR is applied where many simultaneous decisions actually occur. **The synthetic null is itself validated (v6):** before any FDR number is trusted, Service C checks that the synthetic null's p-values are ~uniform **and** that its cross-decision **dependency structure** matches the real corpus's (a BY assumption); a failed check blocks the FDR claim. *(Confidence discounts are not a substitute for error-rate control.)*
- **Dual-γ regression = censored errors-in-variables.** Regress log t50 on log(driving force) with: per-point bootstrap t50 error **and** concentration error; **m_crit's uncertainty propagated** (it is jointly estimated, so resampling captures its correlation with the slope — no plug-in generated-regressor); **near-zero driving-force points down-weighted/flagged** (log of a near-zero noisy quantity is the dominant pathology); **censored points contribute likelihood inequalities**; and the **non-informative-censoring assumption is tested** (censoring-rate vs concentration) — if violated, the γ sub-regime call is **blocked**.
- **Identifiability:** report **identifiable parameter combinations** via FIM eigendecomposition (license stiff combinations even when individual rates are refused). Separate **structural** identifiability (symbolic/global, computed once per model) from **practical** (per-dataset). **Only practical non-identifiability is actionable by M9.** "Down-weight" is an explicit, frozen, versioned monotone function of a named identifiability statistic — not a free knob.
- **Calibration verification:** every emitted confidence is checked against Service C synthetic ground truth via **reliability diagrams + ECE + Brier score**, with **isotonic/Platt recalibration** applied before emission. A "confidence 0.8" must mean ~80% correct. **These calibration numbers are synthetic-validated only**; their transfer to real data is inferred (bounded by the §6C adversarial + artifact-realism + blind real checks), not measured — every emitted confidence carries this ceiling (Invariant 18). *(Cheap, high-value; converts ordinal confidences into operational probabilities.)*

## 6. Cross-cutting services

### Service A — Quality, Confidence & Comparability Guard
- End-to-end flag flow; **hard gates:** block cross-condition cohort normalization, cross-`engine_build_id` comparison, and confident regimes on unfittable/non-identifiable data.
- **Missingness-vs-mismatch policy:** distinguish missing-assumed-default / missing-unknown / explicitly-different. Missing **comparability-critical** field → degrade confidence + widen strata (never silently block — which collapses cohorts to n=1 — nor merge — which corrupts). The design states the unavoidable tension openly: **at the real data volume you can have comparability or n>1, rarely both; PRISE chooses comparability and reports "N too small" honestly.**

### Service B — Repository, Anchors & Reference Scales
- The bundle (§1): the CPAD 2.0 kinetics layer, sequence/annotation layer, and structure-linkage layer (565 structures + PDB cross-refs). **Per-stratum corpus anchors where effective-n suffices** (leakage-free, with anchor CI + basin-stability; §7–§8) **plus** the **versioned physical/synthetic reference scale** as the fallback anchor (§3-M6). Honest missingness/coverage audit (§8) — incl. the concentration-series and fine-grained-condition audits (W3). Stratified default sampling for browsing. Exposed only via `RepositoryLoader`; no live access (CPAD is fetched/parsed at build time, then frozen + versioned).

### Service C — Validation & Identifiability Engine (de-circularized)
- Synthetic curves + realistic noise/artifacts/censoring → parameter-recovery, model-confusion + **data-regime-conditional** degeneracy matrices, identifiability profiling; **calibration verification** (§4).
- **Calibration is now two-source (v7):** *(i)* **synthetic** curves with known ground truth give parameter-recovery, FDR-null calibration + dependency checks, degeneracy maps, and coverage/ECE/Brier with full statistical power for regimes/conditions (incl. those under-represented in CPAD); *(ii)* the **real CPAD kinetics corpus** supports held-out / leave-one-study-out coverage and calibration checks where strata have adequate n — so the all-important *synthetic→real transfer* is now **measured on real held-out data**, not merely inferred (this resolves the v6 disclosure gap). The two are reported separately and reconciled.
- **Breaks self-reference:** (1) **mismatched-generator adversarial testing** — generate test data from a *different* forward model than the fitter (coarse-grained kinetic Monte Carlo / Smoluchowski, added off-pathway species, empirically-estimated literature noise) and report the **degradation when generator ≠ fitter** *on the map itself*; (2) **external real-literature reality check** — run **blind, once** on the handful of proteins the field considers mechanistically resolved (e.g. specific Aβ42/α-synuclein/insulin/β2-microglobulin studies) and compare verdicts to consensus; disagreement on "resolved" cases falsifies the map. This is a **falsification test on a few decisive cases** (like a thermometer against fixed points), **not** a statistical calibration — and it consumes real proteins reserved **solely** for this role (§8 budget table), never used for anchoring. (3) The **artifact generators are themselves validated** against real flagged traces (do synthetic artifacts resemble real ones distributionally?).
- **Pre-registration:** the synthetic generator and all thresholds are fixed from physics/instrument specs, **not tuned to any data they later judge** (§8).

## 7. Reproducibility & versioning

> **Status (2026-08-05).** The version/threshold/comparability machinery below is built and governed (`engine_build_id`, `thresholds_version`, the signed apply record). The **basin-stability gate is now evaluable**: §2.3 `FitProvenance` is emitted, and each fit carries a verdict of `stable` / `knife_edge` / `not_evaluable` / `no_fit` with the per-start ledger behind it (`engine/fit_provenance.py`; `is_basin_stable()` is the gate as a callable). Two parts remain genuinely unmet and are marked as such rather than quietly closed: **(a)** the *pinned containerized reference environment* (pinned BLAS + thread/reduction policy + vendored libm) does not exist — `env_manifest` is a coarse interpreter tag, so "tolerance-reproducible under the pinned environment" is not yet assertable and the bitwise CI tripwire is only meaningful on one machine; **(b)** 9 of the 13 registry models build a single start, so their basin stability is `not_evaluable` rather than verified — §7 treats that conservatively (unevaluable is not freezable), but it is a narrower guarantee than "basin-stable" implies.

- **Reproducibility (corrected):** anchors and CIs are **reproducible within a declared numerical tolerance** (e.g. within a stated fraction of their own bootstrap CI) **under a pinned, containerized reference environment** (pinned BLAS + thread/reduction policy, pinned RNG algorithm + stream policy, pinned/vendored libm). **Bitwise** identity is asserted **only** within that one pinned environment and used as a **CI regression tripwire**, not a cross-platform scientific guarantee. Anchors are freezable **only if basin-stable** (winning multi-start basin robust to start perturbation); knife-edge fits are refused.
- **Versioning DAG (explicit):** `corpus_version → harness_version → anchor_version → {intrinsic, cohort, surface}`; `{taxonomy_version, harness_version, anchor_version, corpus_version} → M8/M9`. **`thresholds_version` (the calibrated M1/M3/M5 cutoffs) is a first-class version axis** feeding the same DAG — a threshold change invalidates every descendant. A bump in any parent **invalidates and forces rebuild** of all descendants (which receive new versions).
- **Comparability key:** every result carries `engine_build_id = hash(scoring_schema_version, taxonomy_version, harness_version, anchor_version, corpus_version, thresholds_version, env_manifest)`. **Comparability is gated on equal `engine_build_id`** (or an explicit declared compatibility table) — not on eyeballing independent version integers. *(Implemented: applying Service C's calibration bumped `thresholds_version` to `thresholds-1.0` and flipped the id `bc442463dfdf → e660cb452b41`.)*
- **Taxonomy governance is auditable:** every registry mutation is a signed, dated, rationale-bearing record bound to a `taxonomy_version`, storing the triggering anomaly cluster. Reproducibility means "reproducible given `taxonomy_version vN`."
- **Threshold governance is auditable (implemented):** a change to any M1/M3/M5 calibrated cutoff is a **signed, dated, rationale-bearing, version-bound artifact** — `engine/calibrated_thresholds.py` (`thresholds-1.0`), which retains BOTH the previous (`first_pass`) and applied (`calibrated`) value for every threshold plus the Service-C source (held-out AUC, n) and an `APPLY_RECORD` (`applied_at 2026-07-01`, `by "governed apply step"`, `rationale "Service C held-out calibration (service-c-1.0)"`, `source_artifact service_c_calibration.json`). Applying it bumped `engine_build_id` (old results non-comparable), the previous values are retained, and the change is **reversible** (restore `first_pass` + re-bump the version). This is exactly the §7 governed-mutation discipline the design demands, now realised for thresholds as well as the regime taxonomy.
- **Performance budget:** bootstrap × multi-start × global mechanistic fits × the (large, synthetic) calibration battery is potentially large and the Knowles fits are stiff; a build-time compute budget is stated and tractability confirmed (sub-sampling of the synthetic battery, if forced, is itself recorded as it affects calibration estimates).

## 8. Offline calibration & reference scales (build-time)

> Build-time only — never at runtime. **v7: two-source (synthetic + real CPAD), leakage-free.** The v6 "synthetic-only" stance is removed because its justifying premise (a tiny real corpus) was factually false (§0 W1–W2).

### 8.1 Two-source calibration
With the real CPAD 2.0 kinetics corpus available, calibration draws on **both** sources, each doing what it does best:

- **Synthetic (unlimited, known truth).** Parameter recovery, data-regime-conditional degeneracy maps, FDR-null calibration + dependency checks, mismatched-generator adversarial degradation, and coverage/ECE/Brier — with **full statistical power**, including for conditions/regimes **under-represented in CPAD**. Pre-registered from physics/instrument specs, never tuned to data it later judges.
- **Real CPAD (held-out).** Coverage and confidence-calibration checks on **held-out / leave-one-study-out** CPAD data where strata reach adequate n. This **measures the synthetic→real transfer** that v6 could only *infer* — closing the v6 disclosure gap (former Invariant-18 caveat).
- **External reality check (a few decisive cases, blind, once).** Well-characterized proteins (Aβ42, α-syn, insulin, β2m, …) falsify mechanistic *verdicts* against field consensus (§6C) — a falsification test, **disjoint** from anchoring/calibration/demonstrators.

**Leakage discipline (unchanged and now feasible):** no real datum is ever both fit-for-anchoring and scored-against-its-own-anchor; anchors are leave-one-study-out; the external-check proteins are reserved solely for §6C.

### 8.2 Scarce-resource budget (every real dataset → exactly one role)
| Role | Source | Used for | **Never** used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Synthetic calibration** | Service C — unlimited | parameter recovery, FDR null, degeneracy maps, power-rich coverage/ECE/Brier | — |
| **Real held-out calibration** | CPAD strata w/ adequate n (held-out / leave-one-study-out) | measure synthetic→real transfer; real coverage/calibration | judging a datum used to fit its own anchor |
| **Corpus anchors** | CPAD strata w/ adequate n | per-stratum intrinsic/cohort anchors (leakage-free) | the held-out calibration split; the external-check set |
| **External reality check** | ~handful well-characterized real proteins | blind, once: falsify mechanistic verdicts vs consensus (§6C) | anchoring, calibration, demonstrators |
| **Demonstrators** | distinct CPAD kinetic entries | default UX / worked examples | the external-check set |
| **Sequence + structure layers** | CPAD annotations + 565 structures + PDB | sequence axis, structure-linkage, browsing, missingness audit, FDR multiplicity | kinetic anchoring |

### 8.3 Build passes
```
SETUP:   run the §1.4 ETL: fetch + parse (full CSV/JSON) + schema-map + concentration-series group
         + version-freeze CPAD 2.0 (1,781 kinetic curves + sequence + 565 structures) and PDB cross-refs.
         Pre-register Service C generator + ALL thresholds + the fallback reference scale
         from physics/instrument specs (NOT from any data they later judge).
         AUDIT (W3): per-protein concentration-series availability; fine-grained-condition completeness;
                     attach graph-digitization uncertainty to CPAD kinetic values.

PASS 1:  SYNTHETIC → parameter recovery, degeneracy maps, FDR-null + dependency checks,
         coverage/ECE/Brier, mismatched-generator adversarial degradation.   [full power]

PASS 2:  derive per-stratum CORPUS ANCHORS leakage-free (leave-one-study-out, n-gated, CI + basin-stable),
         else fall back to the frozen reference scale. Measure synthetic->real transfer on HELD-OUT CPAD.
         Run the BLIND external reality check (§6C). Build corpus reachability SUMMARY + missingness/
         coverage audit (headline).  Phase-2: full M8 map.

REPORT:  synthetic and real-held-out calibration are reported SEPARATELY and reconciled; mechanistic
         credibility comes from the blind real check; anchor provenance (corpus|reference_scale) is stamped.
```
Anchors, reference scale, thresholds, and the reachability summary are versioned, frozen build artifacts tied to `engine_build_id`. **The calibrated M1/M3/M5 thresholds are now a live governed artifact** (`engine/calibrated_thresholds.py`, `thresholds-1.0`): Service C RECOMMENDS them leakage-free, and the separate signed/dated **apply step** installs them (retaining the previous values, bumping `engine_build_id`) — the recommend/apply split keeps calibration leakage-free while the apply remains an auditable, reversible, human-governed mutation (§7).

## 11. Where the novelty honestly lives

The defensible, genuinely novel contributions are:
1. **Rigorous triage of messy real kinetic data** (M1: artifact gate, censoring algebra, plate-level QC, control-chart drift detection) — underdone in the field and immediately valuable, applied at the scale of CPAD's 83k-point kinetics corpus.
2. **Inference-honest mechanistic analysis**: within-tier, partially-pooled, bootstrap-enveloped selection with reported selection-stability frequencies; **FDR-controlled** calls; confidences **calibrated and transfer-checked on held-out real data** — well above field norm.
3. **Correct, modern kinetics**: saturating secondary nucleation, γ-as-constraint gated on curve shape, censored-EIV dual-γ, jointly-fit m_crit — a rigorous treatment of the field's headline discriminator that most pipelines get wrong.
4. **Structure-linked aggregation insight**: connecting fitted regimes/mechanisms to CPAD's 565 aggregation structures + PDB, with honest IDP/polymorph caveats.
5. **The corpus reachability *summary*** — an honest, falsifiable account of how much of CPAD's accumulated data actually supports each inference level (and how much is blocked by missing metadata) — buildable in Phase 1.
6. **(Phase 2, conditional on external validation)** the full reachability **map** and the experiment recommender.

The honest framing: PRISE's value is **epistemic discipline applied to a real, public, multi-protein kinetic corpus** — analyzing what the data genuinely supports (mechanistic where concentration series exist, descriptive otherwise), never overclaiming.

## 13. Glossary
- **Aggregation kinetics** — how an aggregation signal changes over time.
- **ThT / ThS** — Thioflavin T/S dyes; fluoresce on binding amyloid (cross-β) fibrils. Fibril-specific, but **not guaranteed proportional to fibril mass** (polymorph-, surface-, and concentration-dependent).
- **Turbidity / DLS / SLS** — light-based; size/scatter-sensitive; **not** fibril-specific (respond to amorphous aggregates, droplets, gels).
- **Lag time / t50 (half-time)** — apparent onset delay / time to half-max signal. t50 is the more robust summary; both are definition-dependent.
- **Lag-to-t50 ratio** — dimensionless shape invariant; a rare single-curve mechanistic signal.
- **Nucleation / elongation; primary vs secondary nucleation; fragmentation** — formation of new aggregate nuclei vs growth of existing fibrils; new nuclei from free monomer (primary) vs catalyzed on fibril surfaces (secondary); breakage producing more growing ends (fragmentation).
- **Saturating secondary nucleation** — secondary nucleation slows at high monomer because the fibril surface (finite catalyst) saturates; the dominant cause of **non-constant γ** in well-studied systems.
- **Knowles/Cohen integrated rate laws** — mathematical models linking microscopic rate constants (k_n, k₊, k₂, …) to bulk kinetic curves (basis of AmyloFit).
- **γ (scaling exponent)** — slope of log(t50) vs log(driving force); constrains a **combination** of reaction orders (many-to-one onto mechanism), only locally constant.
- **m_crit (critical concentration) / supersaturation** — solubility limit; driving force is `(m − m_crit)` or `ln(m/m_crit)`; m_crit is hard to measure and **anti-correlated with γ** when co-estimated.
- **Bootstrap (wild / block / BCa)** — resampling for uncertainty; wild handles heteroscedasticity, block handles serial correlation, BCa corrects skew.
- **Post-selection inference** — accounting for the model-selection step when quantifying uncertainty; ignoring it understates uncertainty.
- **Errors-in-variables (EIV) / Deming / total least squares** — regression with error in *both* axes.
- **Censoring (left/right)** — true value known only to lie below/above an observed bound (e.g. a run that ended before plateau → right-censored t50).
- **Identifiability (structural vs practical)** — whether parameters are determinable in principle from the model+design (structural) vs from this noisy dataset (practical).
- **Sloppy model** — model whose parameter sensitivities span many orders of magnitude; few stiff combinations constrained, many directions loose.
- **FDR (Benjamini–Hochberg / Yekutieli)** — control of the expected false-discovery proportion across many tests.
- **Calibration (ECE / Brier / reliability diagram)** — whether stated confidences match observed correctness rates.
- **Partial pooling / hierarchical model** — sharing statistical strength across related units (replicate → protein → stratum).
- **APR (aggregation-prone region)** — a sequence stretch predicted to drive aggregation.
- **TANGO / AGGRESCAN / Waltz / Zyggregator / CamSol** — sequence predictors of (respectively) β-aggregation nucleating segments / per-residue aggregation hotspots / amyloid hexapeptides / aggregation propensity / intrinsic solubility — **different observables at different granularities.**
- **CPAD 2.0** — Curated Protein Aggregation Database (Rawat/Prabakaran et al., *Amyloid* 2020; web.iitm.ac.in/bioinfo2/cpad2). Open-access; kinetics: **82,066 time-kinetics data points in 1,781 records** (~46 pts/curve) across **50+ proteins**, with conditions (concentration, pH, temperature, ionic strength, buffer, additives, assay, mutation, PDB-id) + k_agg; plus **912 APRs**, **2,031 peptides**, **565 aggregation-related structures**; downloadable CSV/JSON. Kinetic values are graph-digitized from the literature ("marginal error").
- **PDB** — Protein Data Bank (structural coordinates; CC0). **IDP** — intrinsically disordered protein. **LLPS** — liquid–liquid phase separation. **Polymorphism** — multiple distinct fibril structures for one sequence.
- **engine_build_id** — hash binding all version axes + corpus + environment; the comparability key.
