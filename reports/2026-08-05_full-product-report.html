M17 shipped — and it derives nothing

Every value the engine emits becomes a node in a directed acyclic evidence graph whose edges are facts the engine already computed. M17 went looking for a record that did not exist

PRISE_DESIGN.md §2.3 specifies a FitProvenance object on every emitted parameter, and §10.12 tagged it [test]-enforced. It was emitted nowhere, and no test enforced it. Not a falsely-passing test — no test at all. The consequence for §7 was sharper than "unenforced": the basin-stability gate was unevaluable, because the evidence it needs was destroyed at fit time.

The sharpest consequence, now measured
m7_assemble.gamma_significant() requires a bootstrap CI to exclude zero before a curve earns the scaling tier of the published information_yield ladder. That CI comes out of M4's γ bootstrap. So an unrecorded RNG stream was gating a published tier count — the 59 curves on the scaling rung of §4's headline table. This was never bookkeeping; it was an unrecorded input to a headline number. It is now recorded (§5).

The fix ships as engine/fit_provenance.py + engine/artifact_invariance.py. It is additive and value-preserving, and that claim is mechanically checked rather than argued: across 9,633 kinetic model-fits, every pre-existing key is byte-identical and only provenance was added. 2What PRISE is & the data foundation

The problem

Amyloid aggregation kinetics (ThT/ThS fluorescence vs time) are the workhorse assay of protein misfolding, but the published record is a minefield: curves are digitized from figures (no ground-truth values), differently normalized, often censored (still rising at the end, or no lag at the start), and reported without the conditions (agitation, seeding) that decide which mechanism is even identifiable. Naïve fitting manufactures false certainty.

The approach

A pipeline of pure functions — data in → JSON-serialisable data out — each of which does one job and declares its own limits. Descriptive shape is always available; mechanism is granted only when every licensing gate passes; and a synthetic-calibration backbone (Service C) measures what the engine can actually recover so its confidences are earned, not asserted.

Data foundation

  • CPAD 2.0 kinetics — 1,654 curves, 105 proteins, 52 concentration-series groups (counted from curves_triaged.jsonl), with concentration/pH/temperature/assay/construct metadata. Attribution-gated & non-redistributable → the corpus is .gitignored and lives server-side only; the browser receives derived results, never bulk records.
  • CPAD sequence layer (ETL cpad-sequence-1.1) — TANGO / AGGRESCAN / PASTA / Waltz predictors, APRs, and PDB cross-references per UniProt.
  • PDB coordinates (CC0) — fetched on demand into a local cache; real 3D structural features are computed from them (§11, M16).

3The epistemic discipline (the product's whole point)

Never manufacture certainty

  • Censoring is first-class. A right-censored (still-rising) curve yields a t50 lower bound, never a plateau-normalized point; a left-censored curve reports t50 as biased-early with lag undefined.
  • Mechanism is gated, not guessed. M5 returns an equivalence class and every gate it failed — it never names one mechanism when the data can't separate them.
  • Confidence is calibrated or null. A verdict that isn't licensed carries a null confidence, not a fabricated number.
  • An absent quantity is recorded as absent, with a reason. New this wave: a fit that draws no random number records rng: not applicable and why, rather than writing rng_seed: 0. A fabricated provenance record is a lie that passes — strictly worse than the honest absence it replaces.

Everything is versioned & reproducible

  • engine_build_id hashes every comparability axis (module versions, thresholds, corpus digest, environment). Cross-build comparison is blocked unless the ids match — and is not bumped for a change that moves no value (§7).
  • Governed thresholds. Cutoffs are Service-C-calibrated with a signed/dated apply record; the previous value is retained and reversible.
  • Deterministic. Seeded generation + frozen splits → byte-identical re-runs.
  • Validity ceiling. Phase-2 headlines are explicitly upper bounds on reachability, conditional on forward-model adequacy.
The discipline turned on itself — the lesson of this wave
A [test] tag is worth exactly the negative controls behind it. §10.12 carried a [test] tag for months with no test at all behind it, and nothing in the suite noticed, because a missing test cannot fail. The replacement is written the other way round: the new suite asserts not only that the knife-edge detector fires on a fit that genuinely bifurcates, but that it does not fire on a clean one, that single-start models report not_evaluable and never stable, and that M2 never fabricates an rng_seed. A detector that never fires would pass every positive test while stamping stable on exactly the fits §7 exists to refuse.

5The FitProvenance findingnew

This is the most consequential content in this report. It has three parts: what was found, what it cost, and what the fix measures.

5.1 What was found

An invariant carrying a [test] tag it never earned
PRISE_DESIGN.md §2.3 specifies FitProvenance {rng_seed, rng_algorithm, stream_policy, n_starts, winning_basin, basin_spread, optimizer, version, tolerance, convergence_flag, env_manifest_ref} on every emitted parameter, and §10 invariant 12 tagged it [test]. No engine module constructed it and no artifact contained its fields — those names returned zero hits across engine/, etl/, web/ and every artifact in data/processed/. And it was not a falsely-passing test — there was no test at all: every provenance match in the engine suite was either M1's field_provenance (metadata presence, an unrelated concept) or M15's claim provenance. Surfaced while building M17, which went looking for the record every parameter is supposed to carry; recorded honestly in commit a6c9f88 before any fix was attempted.

What the engine did emit as method provenance — module version tags, engine_build_id, and per-fit converged/param_se — is enough to identify which code produced a number, and not enough to reproduce it.

5.3 The fix, and why it is shaped the way it is

Two halves. §2.3's fields split cleanly into a part that is constant for a fitting policy (optimizer, tolerances, RNG applicability, version, env_manifest_ref) and a part that varies per fit. Inlining the constant half on 9,633 model-fits would say the same sentence 9,633 times, so it is emitted once into data/processed/fit_recipes.json and referenced by recipe id — the shape §2.3 already uses for env_manifest_ref: a ref, not an inlined manifest. Five recipes ship (m2/trf-bounded/det-starts-1.0, mechanistic/…, global_fit/…, m3/wild-bootstrap-mammen-1.0, m4/gamma-bootstrap-1.0). A referenced record is only honest if the reference resolves, so a test asserts every emitted id exists in the table.

Cost was measured, not estimated — and the estimate was wrong. A first pass grew fits.jsonl by 76%, not the ~26% predicted. Two fixes: the explanatory prose became a reason_code resolved through the recipe table, and winning_basin/ basin_spread are omitted in the single-basin case where the first is derivable and the second has no rival to measure against. Measured final cost: 4,781,224 → 6,705,955 bytes (+40.2%), ~200 B per model-fit — confirmed by comparing the live artifact against data/snapshots/pre_fitprovenance/.

SSE alone cannot decide basin stability — the load-bearing measurement, and the one that is easy to get wrong. Two starts reaching the same optimum differ in SSE by ~1e-11; that is the definition of stable. A knife-edge is the opposite: near-equal SSE at a materially different parameter vector, where which vector "wins" is a numerical accident of start ordering. So converged starts are clustered into basins by a scale-free parameter-vector distance

$$ d\big(\theta^{(a)},\theta^{(b)}\big)\;=\;\max_j\; \frac{\big|\theta^{(a)}_j-\theta^{(b)}_j\big|} {\max\big(|\theta^{(a)}_j|,\,|\theta^{(b)}_j|,\,\epsilon_j\big)} $$

and SSE is compared only between basins, relative to a denominator floored at a billionth of the data's total sum of squares — so a near-perfect fit is still compared on a dimensionally meaningful scale rather than against an arbitrary absolute epsilon.

Four verdicts, no fifth: stable · knife_edge · not_evaluable · no_fit. is_basin_stable() returns False for not_evaluable: being unable to check is not permission.

Both tolerances (BASIN_SSE_REL_TOL, BASIN_PARAM_REL_TOL = 1e-3) live in fit_provenance.py and deliberately not in calibrated_thresholds.py: that module is the signed, dated registry of Service-C held-out-calibrated scientific cutoffs, and filing a numerical tolerance there would falsely imply Service-C provenance and drag thresholds_version — and therefore engine_build_id — along for no reason.

5.6 Honesty in both directions

A record that says "not applicable", with a reason
M2, mechanistic and global_fit use no random number generator — their starts are constructed deterministically from the data and TRF is a deterministic descent. Their record therefore carries rng: {applicable: false, reason: …} and no rng_seed key under any value. Writing rng_seed: 0 would state that a stream was opened at seed 0, which is false, and would satisfy a naive §10.12 check while making the record less truthful than the absence it replaced. global_fit's decorative seed is now recorded machine-readably as seed_is_effective: false with the reason attached, and the no-op np.random.seed() line was removed rather than kept — a decorative guarantee is worse than none.

M3's wild bootstrap and M4's γ bootstraps do draw, and carry a real seed, algorithm and stream_policy — where the policy states its own known defect: every curve restarts from the same constant seed 0, so two curves of equal length receive identical Mammen weight sequences rather than independent ones. Deterministic, but correlated across curves. Recorded, and deferred (fixing it moves every published CI).

6M17 — Explanation, Provenance & Evidence Graph (Stage A) m17_explain.pynew

Role. Every value the engine emits becomes a node in a directed acyclic evidence graph whose edges are facts the engine already computed. It answers "why does PRISE say this?" from artifacts on disk, never from a fresh calculation. Version m17-explain-1.0, commit b978025.

The load-bearing invariant: M17 derives nothing
No fitting, no statistics, no thresholds, no number of its own — a harvester, a graph builder and a deterministic renderer. The reason is not stylistic: if M17 could compute, its explanations could disagree with the engine, and an explanation that contradicts the thing it explains is worse than none. This is enforced mechanically, four ways, so a violation fails rather than being discouraged.
GuardWhat it actually does
Value provenance assert_no_derivation() byte-compares every non-null node value against the artifact value at its declared key path, re-resolved against the artifact re-read from disk — not the pruned in-memory bundle, so pruning cannot hide a bad reference. A number M17 invented exists at no key path, so it cannot pass.
Arithmetic ban _frac() is the only function in the module containing a division, multiplication, subtraction or power — and it divides M17's own node counts, never an engine value. The suite parses the file with ast and asserts it.
Template-only rendering render_node() is one dict lookup plus one str.format_map, with no concatenation, f-string or join — a sentence cannot be composed at runtime. Same graph ⇒ byte-identical text.
No numpy Stdlib only, so every float arrives via json.loads as a plain Python float and no numpy.float64 repr can drift between builds.

Adapter contract

151 declared key paths checked, 0 failures. When an upstream module changes shape the M17 suite fails loudly instead of silently degrading every explanation to explanation_available: false.

Cross-artifact value identity

0 mismatched of 132,292 checked. M17 nodes read the originating artifact, not the assembled M7 spine — so this proves the explained number is the number the product displays.

Determinism

Consecutive full runs are byte-identical by SHA-256. The 90 MB full graph dump is therefore not written by default: a regenerable dump beats a stored one, and web/server.py indexes large per-series JSONLs into memory at startup.

Default outputs are explanation_coverage.json plus a deterministic stratified sample, explanations_sample.jsonl — verified today as 9 records: a manifest, one __corpus__ graph, and 7 series graphs (both worked-example anchors, one per distinct M5 gates_failed signature, the first γ-joined series, and the first series with no M5 record; ties broken lexicographically).

7Why engine_build_id deliberately does not bumpnew

This report is generated against e660cb452b41 — the same build id as the 2026-07-21 report, and that is a deliberate governance decision, not an oversight.

engine_build_id is a hash over every comparability axis, and §7 gates cross-build comparison on it: results from different ids are not directly comparable. That makes a bump a positive assertion — it says "the numbers moved; do not compare these to the previous run." The FitProvenance change is additive and value-preserving: it writes down quantities the fitting loops had already computed and discarded. No fitted value moved. So bumping the id would assert a non-comparability that does not exist, invalidating an entire corpus of correct, still-comparable results for a change that moved nothing. Under §7's own logic, that is as much a false statement as failing to bump when values do move.

The argument is not accepted on its own terms. "An argument is not a proof, and reasoning is precisely what M17 just taught this codebase not to trust." So the claim is checked mechanically by artifact_invariance.py (§5.5), which fails on any pre-existing value that moved or vanished and on any new key outside a four-name allow-list. Verified today: 0 violations across 9,633 model-fits in fits.jsonl and all 55 in dose_response_fits.jsonl. All five artifacts verified 2026-08-06: all_ok: true, 0 violations.

Applying Service C's calibrated thresholds did move published values, and correctly flipped the id bc442463dfdf → e660cb452b41, marking every first-pass result non-comparable. M4 — Feature Extractor, Definition Contract & Dual-γ m4_features.py

Role. Turn selected models into interpretable quantities under a versioned definition contract (m4-defs-1.0) — because a Gompertz t50 and a tangent-intercept lag are only comparable across proteins when computed the same way.

Dual-γ — the headline mechanistic constraint for a concentration series:

15Honest gaps, discrepancies & deferrals

Gap A — §2.3 / §10.12 / §7: what is now met, and what is still not
Met: FitProvenance is emitted by every module that runs an optimizer; §10.12's [test] tag is now earned by test_fit_provenance.py (with negative controls, not just positive ones) and test_artifact_invariance.py; §7's basin-stability gate is evaluable and exposed as a callable.

Still not met, stated plainly: (a) §7's pinned containerized reference environment (pinned BLAS + thread/reduction policy + vendored libm) does not exist — env_manifest is the coarse interpreter tag python3.13, so "tolerance-reproducible under the pinned reference environment" is not assertable and the bitwise CI tripwire is meaningful on one machine only. (b) 9 of 13 registry models are single-start, so their stability is unevaluated rather than verified — a narrower guarantee than "basin-stable" implies. (c) M3's constant-seed stream policy (every curve restarts from seed 0, so equal-length curves receive identical Mammen weight sequences) is recorded in the provenance and not yet fixed.
Gap B — the single-start fix is deliberately deferred, and the reason is governance
Giving every registry model ≥2 genuine starts is a ~10-line change. It is not being made quietly, because it changes fitted values: a model that currently reports the first constructed start's optimum could land elsewhere. That requires its own governed change — an engine_build_id bump and a full M2→M9 rebuild, marking prior results non-comparable by design. Bundling it into an additive provenance change would have smuggled a value-moving edit under a non-bumping one. This is the mirror image of §7's argument: the id must bump when the science moves, and must not when it doesn't.
Gap C — M17 explains about half of what the engine emits
Coverage is 45.6% of distinct paths, and the two instance figures (51.0% all-series, 49.5% explainable-series) are reported separately and never blended, because they decompose into two populations with different fixes:
