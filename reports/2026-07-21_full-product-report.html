1Executive summary

PRISE turns a corpus of 1,654 digitized amyloid aggregation-kinetics curves (CPAD 2.0, spanning ~105 proteins) into calibrated, provenance-stamped scientific claims. Its distinguishing feature is not any single model — it is a refusal to overclaim: every estimate carries its censoring status, its identifiability, a calibrated confidence, and a validity ceiling. The engine is complete through Module 16 plus a full cross-cutting validation stack, and is surfaced through a working web application.

The single most important corpus finding is a piece of honesty made quantitative: of 1,654 curves, 0 support a single-mechanism claim — not because the models fail, but because the two metadata fields that gate mechanism (agitation and seeding) are unrecorded corpus-wide. 2What PRISE is & the data foundation

The problem

Amyloid aggregation kinetics (ThT/ThS fluorescence vs time) are the workhorse assay of protein misfolding, but the published record is a minefield: curves are digitized from figures (no ground-truth values), differently normalized, often censored (still rising at the end, or no lag at the start), and reported without the conditions (agitation, seeding) that decide which mechanism is even identifiable. Naïve fitting manufactures false certainty.

The approach

A pipeline of pure functions — data in → JSON-serialisable data out — each of which does one job and declares its own limits. Descriptive shape is always available; mechanism is granted only when every licensing gate passes; and a synthetic-calibration backbone (Service C) measures what the engine can actually recover so its confidences are earned, not asserted.

Data foundation

  • CPAD 2.0 kinetics — 1,654 curves, ~105 proteins, with concentration/pH/temperature/assay/ construct metadata. Attribution-gated & non-redistributable → the corpus is .gitignored and lives server-side only; the browser receives derived results, never bulk records.
  • CPAD sequence layer (ETL cpad-sequence-1.1) — TANGO / AGGRESCAN / PASTA / Waltz predictors, APRs, and PDB cross-references per UniProt.
  • PDB coordinates (CC0) — fetched on demand into a local cache; real 3D structural features are computed from them (§M16).

3The epistemic discipline (the product's whole point)

Never manufacture certainty

  • Censoring is first-class. A right-censored (still-rising) curve yields a t50 lower bound, never a plateau-normalized point; a left-censored curve reports t50 as biased-early with lag undefined.
  • Mechanism is gated, not guessed. M5 returns an equivalence class and every gate it failed — it never names one mechanism when the data can't separate them.
  • Confidence is calibrated or null. A verdict that isn't licensed carries a null confidence, not a fabricated number.

Everything is versioned & reproducible

  • engine_build_id hashes every comparability axis (module versions, thresholds, corpus digest, environment). Cross-build comparison is blocked unless the ids match.
  • Governed thresholds. Cutoffs are Service-C-calibrated with a signed/dated apply record; the previous value is retained and reversible.
  • Deterministic. Seeded generation + frozen splits → byte-identical re-runs.
  • Validity ceiling. Phase-2 headlines are explicitly upper bounds on reachability, conditional on forward-model adequacy.

M2 — Model-Fitting Bank m2_fit.py · models.py · mechanistic.py

Role. Fit (only) a bank of models on three axes; selection is M3, mechanism is M5.

  • Kinetic (signal vs time): linear-no-threshold, logistic, Gompertz, Richards (generalised-logistic), exponential, power/scaling, Brain–Cousens (hormesis), Finke–Watzky.
  • Dose–response (response vs conc): LNT, threshold (hockey-stick), Hill 4PL, Brain–Cousens, scaling.
  • Tier-B mechanistic (ODE): one Knowles/Cohen moment framework covering nucleation-elongation, secondary-nucleation, saturating-secondary, fragmentation — integrated with LSODA, fit in log-rate space.

Each fit reports SSE / \(R^2\) / AIC / AICc / BIC and parameter SEs. Candidates are routed by M1's handling (a sigmoid is never forced onto a non-monotonic curve). The Tier-B ODE fits are sloppy on one curve — individual rates aren't identifiable — which is exactly why global concentration fitting (below) exists.

M3 — Comparison, Selection & Identifiability m3_select.py

Role. The statistical spine. Model selection runs inside the bootstrap (Mammen wild resampling): each resample re-fits and re-selects, so PRISE reports honest selection-stability frequencies instead of pretending the winner was known.

Per curve: Akaike weights \(w_i = e^{-\Delta_i/2}\big/\sum_j e^{-\Delta_j/2}\); a cross-model t50 predictive interval (pooled only over the model-transferable quantity, flagged when selection is multimodal); per-model conditional CIs (never pooled across models); and an identifiability diagnostic — the condition number of the column-normalised sensitivity (correlation) matrix, with a practical-non-identifiability flag at the Service-C-calibrated cutoff COND_NUMBER_NONIDENT = 107 (was a round-guess 1000).

Deferred & documented: block/sieve bootstrap for serial correlation, BCa intervals, corpus-scale FDR.

M4 — Feature Extractor, Definition Contract & Dual-γ m4_features.py

Role. Turn selected models into interpretable quantities under a versioned definition contract (m4-defs-1.0) — because a Gompertz t50 and a tangent-intercept lag are only comparable across proteins when computed the same way.

Single-curve features (read off the selected model on a dense grid): t50 (primary), tangent-intercept lag, the dimensionless lag-to-t50 ratio, inflection time, max rate, t10/t90, transition sharpness, plateau, dynamic range — each censoring-aware and with a non-cooperative guard (a line/saturation has no interior inflection → lag returned None, never a fake argmax).

Dual-γ — the headline mechanistic constraint for a concentration series, with the half-time scaling exponent defined by

$$ t_{50}(m)\;\propto\;m^{-\gamma}, \qquad \log t_{50} = c - \gamma\,\log m. $$

Two separately-provenanced, never-merged estimators: (1) gamma_regression — a censored log–log regression (right-censored curves contribute an inequality \(P(Y\ge \text{bound})\), not a point), with a curvature test, an informative-censoring test, an mcrit identifiability diagnostic, and a negative-γ driving-force gate; (2) gamma_global — a phenomenological master-curve collapse coupling every curve through one shared logistic shape in rescaled time \(\tau=t/t_{50}(m)\). An operational disagreement test always reports \(|\gamma_{\text{reg}}-\gamma_{\text{collapse}}|\) against a pre-registered margin (0.3). γ is a constraint, not a discriminator.

Live corpus finding
Of 1,240 fittable curves, 690 are genuinely cooperative (interior inflection). Across the 532 with a defined tangent-intercept lag, the lag-to-t50 ratio clusters at median 0.70 — the textbook signature of nucleation-dependent amyloid kinetics, surfaced as a corpus result. Over the 52 concentration series, 77 γ fits run cleanly and 59 resolve a scaling exponent (95% CI excludes 0); e.g. Aβ42 \(\gamma_{\text{reg}}\approx1.07\) (CI 0.94–1.14) vs \(\gamma_{\text{global}}\approx1.10\).

global_fit — Shared-rate Knowles/Cohen ODE global fit global_fit.py

Role. The real AmyloFit-grade fit the design promised. For a concentration series it fits all member curves simultaneously with shared microscopic parameters \(\{\log_{10}k_n,\log_{10}k_+,\log_{10}k_2, n_c, n_2, [K_M]\}\) — reaction orders estimated, not hard-fixed at 2 — with only the monomer concentration varying per curve. Residuals from every curve stack into one vector; fit with bounded TRF least-squares, multi-start, per mechanism family, AICc-ranked.

It emits an observed Fisher Information identifiability report

$$ \mathcal{I} = S^{\mathsf T}\Sigma^{-1}S, \qquad \Sigma=\sigma^2 I, $$

eigen-decomposed into identifiable combinations vs sloppy directions, plus an analytic third \(\gamma_{\text{mech}}\) from the fitted orders (Meisl/Knowles: \(\gamma=\tfrac{n_2+1}{2}\) secondary, \(\tfrac{n_c+1}{2}\) primary, \(\tfrac12\) fragmentation). Honesty: these fits are sloppy on this corpus — only stiff rate combinations are identifiable, so the value is the reaction-order constraint, and it does not license a single mechanism.

E.g. ABeta40 series → best family nucleation-elongation, \(n_c\approx2.96\), \(\gamma_{\text{mech}}\approx1.98\), \(R^2\approx0.99\), sloppy (1 stiff / 2 sloppy directions).

pooling — Empirical-Bayes partial-pooling spine pooling.py

Role. The §4 hierarchical layer: shrink noisy single-curve estimates toward their condition+assay-matched stratum mean by a frozen, versioned, monotone function of each estimate's own sampling variance — deterministic empirical-Bayes (James–Stein), closed-form, no MCMC. Two-level Gaussian with a DerSimonian–Laird moment \(\tau^2\):

$$ \hat\theta_i^{\text{EB}} = \bar\theta + B_i(\hat\theta_i-\bar\theta), \qquad B_i = \frac{\tau^2}{\tau^2+s_i^2}. $$

Cross-condition pooling is forbidden (Service-A discipline). This is the defensible first layer of the spine, not a full latent-rate ODE hierarchy (documented as a deferred extension).

M5 — Classification & Regime Registry m5_classify.py

Role. Emit three things against a declarative, versioned REGIME_REGISTRY (m5-regimes-1.0): a descriptive regime (pure shape, always available), a mechanistic equivalence class + failed gates (never a single mechanism), and a model-agnostic anomaly detector (Wald–Wolfowitz runs test on residuals → corpus Benjamini–Hochberg FDR, plus a material-misfit \(R^2<0.9\) gate so near-perfect curves don't flood the queue).

M6 — Propensity Scorer & Sequence Axis m6_propensity.py

Role. A propensity statement in three never-collapsed views — surface (feature-vs-driving-force + γ), intrinsic (a leakage-free corpus anchor, query excluded from its own stratum, else a versioned reference scale), and cohort (a Hazen mid-rank percentile, self-excluded, suppressed for singletons). Because low t50 = high propensity, every record carries an explicit direction and a sign-corrected percentile so a consumer can't sort backwards.

The sequence axis is de-conflated: TANGO / AGGRESCAN / PASTA / Waltz as separate named sub-axes, and a comparison is licensed only on endpoint match — vs an amyloid dye (ThT/ThS/Congo Red) only the amyloid-specific PASTA & Waltz are licensed; TANGO & AGGRESCAN (generic β-aggregation) are barred; an unknown assay licenses nothing.

Live: 151 protein×construct records — 119 with a sequence axis, 126 with structure linkage, 108 corpus-anchored, 39 censored-only-suppressed, 65 with a γ surface. Only point t50 enters ranked pools; right-censored proteins are emitted but intrinsic/cohort suppressed (a "≥" value isn't rankable).

Cross-cutting validation stack

Service C — Synthetic Calibration & Identifiability service_c.py

Role. The de-circularized backbone. It generates curves from a known ground truth under a pre-registered noise/artifact/censoring law (fixed from physics/instrument specs, never tuned to the data it later judges), runs the real engine on them, and measures what the engine recovers — deterministic (seeded → byte-identical).

  • Parameter recovery — bias / RMSE / Wald-95% coverage per parameter and t50.
  • The concentration-series advantage, measured (K2): a single curve collapses all four Tier-B mechanisms into one equivalence class; a γ-resolved multi-concentration family splits them into four singletons. This is the quantitative basis of the M8/M9 boundary.
  • FDR-null with a power arm — realized false-discovery-proportion 0 with demonstrated power on a bank-unrepresentable alternative (control only claimed where power is shown).
  • Calibration — held-out threshold recommendations (out-of-sample AUC reported; a test proves the leakage mechanism on pure noise) → the governed apply step.
  • De-circularization (C1) — a mismatched generator (ODE truth vs closed-form fitter) is reported as mechanism-misidentification degradation: matched mechanism-ID accuracy 0.667, mismatched 0.0, yet coarse-regime accuracy holds at 0.933 — the engine degrades on mechanism exactly where it should, not on shape.

Deferred: an external forward-model (KMC/Smoluchowski) generator, artifact-realism distributional check, synthetic→real transfer.

conformal — Split / Mondrian Conformal Prediction conformal.py

Role. Finite-sample-valid, distribution-free coverage — the thing bootstrap percentile intervals under-deliver on this small/noisy/censored corpus. Calibrated on Service-C synthetic curves (the only ground truth), running the real engine on each. For a continuous target the split-conformal score is \(s=|\hat t_{50}-t_{50}|\); the interval uses the empirical quantile

$$ q = \Big\lceil (n+1)(1-\alpha)\Big\rceil\text{-th of the calibration scores},\qquad \big[\hat t_{50}-q,\ \hat t_{50}+q\big]\ \text{covers} \ge 1-\alpha. $$

Mondrian: the quantile is stratified by the same tags the engine already emits (censoring × assay × regime) so coverage holds within strata; right-censored t50 becomes a one-sided \([\hat t_{50}-q,\infty)\). The regime classifier yields prediction sets with the same guarantee (set size > 1 only when evidence is genuinely split).

Live validation (α = 0.1, target 0.90)
t50 marginal realized coverage 0.974 (mean width 7.8 h, 110 one-sided); regime marginal coverage 0.915 (mean set size 2.28) — both ≥ target. Per-stratum quantiles are much tighter than the pooled marginal.

Honesty: calibrated on synthetic truth; real-data coverage holds insofar as the pre-registered noise/censoring law matches the digitized traces — a stated, testable assumption.

reality_check — Blind External Falsification reality_check.py

Role. The de-circularizing falsification test — a thermometer against fixed points, not a statistical calibration. Run blind, once, on the handful of proteins the field considers mechanistically resolved (reserved solely for this role, never used for anchoring): does PRISE's honest output contradict consensus?

Live result
5 / 5 fixed points CONSISTENT, 0 contradictions (Aβ42, Aβ40, α-synuclein, insulin, β2-microglobulin). PRISE's honest refusal to name a single mechanism (agitation/seeding unknown) does not contradict the field on any resolved case it can see. This is a non-contradiction reading on a tiny n — not proof of correctness.

M8 — Inferential Reachability Map m8_reachability.py

Role. Consolidate existing artifacts into a falsifiable map of what is inferable from CPAD's bulk kinetics, and what is not — no new inference. Every headline carries the conditioning clause "reachability under the PRISE forward model" and a validity ceiling.

The boundary (the headline). A structural upper bound of 33 proteins / 48 concentration series are rich enough that Service C shows a γ-series could resolve mechanism — vs 0 actually licensed → GAP = 33. Blocker census (impact-ranked): agitation/seeding unknown 1,654 (100%) · censoring 1,020 (62%) · no concentration series 1,005 (61%).

M8 consumes M11 to make the map causal: median metadata_score is 0.69 but median mechanistic-completeness is only 0.4 — the corpus records the conditions but not the mechanism-gating {agitation, seeding}, which is why the gap is unresolved. M9 — Experimental-Design Recommender m9_recommender.py

Role. For each unit, the experiment that would most reduce practical degeneracy, ranked by reduction ÷ cost, from a Service-C-distilled rule table whose gains are quantified by Service C's measured degeneracy reduction (the 1→4 Tier-B split — a measured ~4×). It is dependency-honest: recording agitation/seeding only unblocks mechanism where a series already exists, and is discounted + flagged elsewhere. Full EIG is deferred to M13.

Live headline
Recording agitation & seeding is the single cheapest action that closes the M8 GAP — it unblocks the mechanistic license for the 33 series-bearing structural-reachable proteins (48 series). On single-curve units it only removes a future gate; there the binding prerequisite is a concentration series.

M10 — Cross-modal Sequence × Kinetics × Structure m10_crossmodal.py

Role. PRISE uniquely co-holds sequence predictors, measured kinetics, and structure/APR for the same proteins. M10 asks the honest question: does a sequence-only predictor actually predict the measured rate/regime? — at protein-level rigor (effective n = proteins, not curves).

The binding honesty constraint — a variance ceiling. A sequence-only predictor is a per-protein constant; it cannot explain any within-protein t50 variance. Live: the between-protein fraction is only 0.32, so the most any sequence predictor could explain is ~32% — the other 68% is condition-driven. Discordance (predictor vs measured) fires on just 2 of 22 proteins (9.1%).

Coverage: 45 proteins / 1,433 curves; effective n ≈ 20 proteins; PASTA n=20, Waltz n=14.

M11 — Metadata Quality & Information Completeness m11_metadata.py

Role. Formalize and quantify the metadata layer the engine already licenses inference against — every gate M11 reasons about mirrors a real M5/M8 gate. Per series it computes a weighted metadata_score \(=\sum_f w_f\,\text{present}(f)\big/\sum_f w_f\) (mechanism-gating fields weighted high), a separate uncertainty scalar, and a per-missing-field Δ-inferential-power (which tiers it blocks + the tier-lift it would enable, labelled service_c_measured where anchored).

Live (105 proteins)
Median metadata_score 0.685 (nothing below 0.52, nothing above 0.79 — the corpus records conditions consistently) but median mechanistic_completeness 0.40 — the mechanism-gating fields are the systematic hole. Completeness ≠ correctness, and information-gain numbers are structural upper bounds except the Service-C-measured ones.

M12 — Information Content & Experimental-Design Geometry m12_information.py

Role. Unify the information geometry the pipeline already touches into one per-curve treatment — consistent with, not a third opinion of, M3/M4/global_fit. Given a fitted curve it computes the sensitivity Jacobian \(S\), the observed FIM \(\mathcal I = S^{\mathsf T}\Sigma^{-1}S\), its eigen-spectrum (effective rank, condition number, spectral entropy), per-parameter observability (CRLB \(\text{SE}\ge\sqrt{[\mathcal I^{-1}]_{jj}}\)), the per-point information-density profile \(D(t)\) by lag/growth/plateau phase, redundancy \(n_{\text{eff}}\) vs \(n\), and a Sherman–Morrison optimal-next-timepoint scan.

Live (1,240 curves)
Median effective rank 4.0 = median parameter count 4.0 (only 5.3% sloppy); median information-richness 0.57; redundancy 0.28; and 87% of curves carry more information in the growth phase than the plateau — so the modal recommended action is sample denser at the transition.

M13 — Bayesian Optimal Experimental Design (BOED) m13_boed.py

Role. The rigorous BOED capstone of the design thread (M9 rules → M12 geometry → M13 multi-variable BOED). It scores candidate next experiments ξ by expected information gain, reusing M12's exact FIM machinery on prospective designs. For the linear-Gaussian/Laplace regime the EIG is a D-optimality log-det:

$$ \text{EIG}(\xi)\;\approx\;\tfrac12\log\det\!\big(\mathcal I_{\text{prior}}+\mathcal I(\xi)\big) -\tfrac12\log\det\mathcal I_{\text{prior}}, $$

ranked by EIG ÷ cost, with a non-dominated Pareto frontier and a greedy K-step batch whose marginal gains diminish (submodular log-det). It is honest that its 8 design variables are of two kinds — quantitative (concentration, T, pH, replicate count, sampling frequency/duration: a real simulated \(\mathcal I(\xi)\)) and ones needing the self-similar-shape approximation (stated).

Live: 839 units (49 series, 790 single-curve). Top recommendation distribution — increase_measurement_frequency 482 · extend_sampling_duration 241 · add_concentration_series 86 · record_agitation_and_seeding 23.

M14 — Cross-Study Hierarchical Meta-Analysis m14_meta.py

Role. Generalize the replicate-level random-effects meta (pooling.py) up to the study level (one publication = one study). Because raw t50 can't be pooled across concentrations, it is a meta-regression on condition-adjusted log-t50:

$$ \hat\theta_c = \mu + \beta\,x_{\text{condition}} + u_{\text{study}} + \varepsilon_c,\qquad u_{\text{study}}\sim N(0,\tau^2_{\text{between}}), $$

with \(\tau^2_{\text{between}}\) by DerSimonian–Laird (Paule–Mandel refinement) + BLUP shrinkage, and — under weak priors — a posterior-predictive interval for a new study. This is not a full latent-ODE MCMC hierarchy (documented deferral).

Live: 60 proteins; 11 have ≥2 studies and receive a meta-analysis; 49 are single-study. The web app renders forest / funnel / heterogeneity panels per protein.

M15 — Literature Ingestion & Claim Extraction m15_litingest.py

Role. Turn papers into structured, traceable claims — never trusted facts. Every extraction is a Claim with full provenance (doi + page + char-span + verbatim text), a calibrated confidence, and an entry in an append-only immutable ledger — a correction appends a superseding claim, and divergent values across papers surface as conflict records (both provenances kept, never auto-resolved). Accuracy is measured against CPAD, not asserted.

Live validation vs curated CPAD (PMID 18258258)
Precision / recall / accuracy = 1.00 / 1.00 / 1.00 (tp 6, fp 0, fn 0) on the extracted conditions; 2 conflicts logged. Honesty: the PDF/OCR/NER/figure-digitize front-ends are declared pluggable stubs — the offline environment has no pdfminer/PyMuPDF/pytesseract/spacy, so everything downstream of the Document abstraction is deterministic-real on synthetic fixtures, and the front-ends raise a clear NotImplementedError naming the missing dependency (they are not faked).

M16 — Sequence ↔ Structure ↔ Kinetics Bridge m16_structure.py

Role. Extend M10 with a structure-feature axis — do structure-derived features associate with measured kinetics? — at the same protein-level rigor (LOPO CV, permutation nulls, variance ceiling, BH-FDR). The final commit upgraded this from sequence proxies to real 3D features computed from actual PDB coordinates in pure numpy (no BioPython/DSSP/ FreeSASA). Every extractor stamps a fidelity label:

contact_map — Cβ distance matrix, contact order · EXACT real_sasa — Shrake–Rupley rolling-ball (96 sphere pts) · EXACT algorithm secondary_struct — φ/ψ dihedral geometry · APPROXIMATION (not DSSP H-bonding) hydrophobic_patches — SASA-exposed clusters via contact map

Live coverage & result
382 sequence proteins → 45 in kinetics → 14 with a PDB reference → 11 with real 3D features (10 native, 1 fibril = tau/4NP8), all 11 PDB fetches succeeded. 30 association cards (18 real-3D, 12 proxy). The structure→kinetics variance ceiling is 42.7% between-protein. Honestly, the associations are weak: e.g. β-propensity vs lag_time ρ = 0.25, permutation p = 0.25, FDR q = 0.51, LOPO R² negative — i.e. no out-of-sample predictive skill, reported as a null rather than dressed up. And a first-class caveat rides every card: a native-monomer structure is not the fibril state, so any structure→kinetics link is associative and confounded.

The web application

A working stdlib-based HTTP server (web/server.py) + single-page frontend (web/index.html, app.js) exposes the engine over a JSON API: browse proteins (M0), plot a curve with its M1 triage, run an analysis to the assembled ProteinAnalysisResult, and open per-milestone panels — pooling, reachability (M8), information density (M12), the BOED Pareto frontier (M13), the M14 forest/funnel/heterogeneity plots, the M15 claims/conflicts browser, and the M16 structure ↔ kinetics panel. The honesty UI is the differentiator: censoring badges, degeneracy cards, calibrated-confidence-with-its-caveat, and the information_yield badge. The frozen corpus stays server-side (licensing); the browser receives only derived results.

Every milestone has a matching "web:" commit, so the UI tracked the engine — with one exception noted next.

Live discrepancy A — web test fails (engine ↔ web desync)
The final M16 commit (bcb2eba, "real PDB 3D features — no proxies") upgraded beta_sheet_content from a flat proxy to a nested {proxy, real_3d} schema with a has_real_3d flag, but touched only engine/m16_structure.py + its test. So /api/structure now passes through the richer 3D shape, while test_web.py still asserts the old flat is_3d_derived is False → the web test fails today. The engine's own 37 M16 tests all pass; this is a low-severity web-layer lag, not an engine defect. Fix: update the /api/structure serializer + honesty labels + web test to the nested 3D schema (a code change, not part of this report).

Documented within-module deferrals (by design, clearly flagged)

  • M15 front-ends — PDF/OCR/NER/figure-digitize are pluggable stubs (no libs / no PDFs / no network offline); everything downstream is real.
  • M3 — block/sieve bootstrap for serial correlation, BCa intervals, corpus-scale FDR.
  • M4 — a full Knowles/Cohen ODE global γ as the primary estimator (the master-curve collapse is v1), separate left-censoring algebra, an errors-in-variables term for concentration.
  • Service C — an external forward-model (KMC/Smoluchowski) generator, artifact-realism distributional check, synthetic→real transfer.
  • pooling / M14 — a full latent-rate ODE Bayesian hierarchy (the closed-form EB / random-effects layer is the defensible first step).
  • M7 — env_manifest is python3.13 only (OS/BLAS/RNG fingerprints await a pinned container).
  • M13 — EIG beyond the linear-Gaussian/Laplace regime for the non-simulable variables.
The standing scientific ceiling (not a bug — the finding)
0 curves license a single mechanism because agitation & seeding are unrecorded corpus-wide. This is a metadata ceiling: PRISE quantifies exactly which 33 proteins / 48 series would cross it, and that recording two fields is the cheapest way through. Every reachability count is an upper bound conditional on forward-model adequacy (Service C's mismatch degradation is the backing evidence).
